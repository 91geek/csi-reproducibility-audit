【论文核心贡献（200 字摘要）】
WiFi CSI-based cross-domain gesture recognition has attracted intensive research in the past six years, yet reported improvements rarely reproduce under controlled evaluation. We present a paired benchmark covering ten recent algorithmic improvements evaluated under an 18-fold × 5-seed cross-validated protocol on the Widar3.0 benchmark. All ten methods fail to reach significance (|Cohen's d| < 0.3, p > 0.28). Variance decomposition reveals the root cause: within-seed standard deviation (4.82 pp) is 4.7× the largest reported effect. We then introduce a minimal signal-representation modification—expanding the BVP operator to retain the multi-Rx antenna dimension—that yields +8.14 pp (Cohen's d = 1.71, 19/1 wins). We further test this finding on a second dataset MMFi and find it does not generalize—the dataset-optimal representation flips to multi-scale on MMFi. No single BVP representation is universally optimal.
