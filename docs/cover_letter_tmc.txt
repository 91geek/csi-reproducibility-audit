Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "WiFi CSI Cross-Domain Gesture Recognition: A Reproducibility Audit Revealing Dataset-Conditioned Optimality of the Multi-Rx Representation" for consideration as a regular paper in IEEE TMC. This manuscript extends our earlier arXiv pre-print (v9, Sep 29 2026) by adding (i) a closed-form Statistical Foundation (§3.5, Theorems 1--3 + Corollary 1) that converts the audit into a pre-registration exercise, (ii) a System Overhead table (§10.4) benchmarking inference latency for the five audited backbones on a CPU, and (iii) an explicit "30%-subset + segment-level audit" policy recommendation (§9.6) derived from the four-data-point generalization.

SCOPE AND FIT

The manuscript addresses cross-environment generalization of WiFi sensing --- a core topic in mobile and pervasive computing --- through a paired multi-seed reproducibility audit. TMC is the natural venue because (i)~WiFi sensing is a long-standing TMC topic (e.g., AttnSense TMC 2021, MMFi TMC 2023, WiTraj TMC 2021), (ii)~cross-environment robustness is a systems-level concern that goes beyond pure algorithmic novelty, and (iii)~methodology papers with rigorous paired evaluations have historically been well-received at TMC (e.g., the recent reproducibility-crisis line of work in adjacent venues).

CONTRIBUTIONS (C1--C5)

C1 (Methodology): A paired leave-one-domain-out × multi-seed × MDE-pre-registered reproducibility audit protocol. We provide closed-form statistical foundations (Section 3.5): Theorem 1 derives the paired sample-size bound n ≥ (z_{1-α/2} + z_{1-β})² σ²/ε²; Theorem 2 derives the MDE closed form; Theorem 3 states the Hedges' g small-sample correction; Corollary 1 combines the three into a three-criterion "real effect" test. Substituting our empirical σ_within ≈ 4.8 pp shows that single-seed reports of ≤ 2 pp gain are *provably underpowered* at any paired design with n ≤ 18 (n ≥ 56 required). The protocol, code, and all paired evaluations are released.

C2 (Empirical Finding): Eleven paired comparisons across Widar3.0 (6 LODO folds × up to 5 seeds) and MMFi (4 environments × 5 seeds) re-evaluate ten method axes spanning algorithmic (DANN, Snapshot×3, Mixup, Reweighting, Pretrain), architectural (LeNet+MHA, ResNetSmall, CBAM+ResNet18 control), and representational (Widar-orig, Multi-scale, Multi-Rx, RMS-agg, Single-Rx) families. Seven of eleven comparisons fail the three-criterion audit (p > 0.28, |d| < 0.3, |Δ| < MDE). Variance decomposition shows within-seed σ ranges from 1.0 pp (RMS-agg) to 8.2 pp (Multi-Rx) — comparable to the largest reported effect, explaining why single-seed studies cannot distinguish real gains from noise.

C3 (Mechanism): The BVP Multi-Rx expansion (retain A = 9 antennas as a tensor axis instead of collapsing via RMS-aggregation) yields +8.14 pp (Cohen's d = 1.71, g = 1.68) on Widar3.0. The gain is attributed to two factors: (i) the antenna participation ratio rises from 1.00 to 1.89, and (ii) the total Fisher information rises by 1.82× (~82%). We verify the mechanism three ways: eigenvalue effective rank, Fisher F per axis, and information density fold.

C4 (Boundary & Honest Reanalysis): The +8.14 pp gain vanishes on MMFi — the dataset-optimal representation *flips* to Multi-scale (+1.20 pp, p = 0.016). t-SNE reveals the geometric root cause: MMFi's four environments are indistinguishable in BVP feature space, so there is no discriminative signal for Multi-Rx to amplify. We then perform a v1→v2 cache reanalysis: the v1 cache (1080 path-labeled samples) was a cache-construction artifact; the v2 segment-level cache (16,447 real action segments) shows the backbone comparison becoming statistically indistinguishable (Δ = -0.33 pp, p = 0.564). On the 50,000-sample Widar3.0 subset, the original 12k backbone comparison (CBAM+ResNet18 -4.61 pp, p = 0.028) *reverses* to +5.75 pp (p = 0.0609, d = +0.44, CI [-0.26, +11.77], 14/18 wins). These four data points generalize the boundary from "dataset-flip" to "dataset-conditioned optimality".

C5 (System Overhead & Reproducibility): A complete computational profile (§10.4) shows the LeNetCSI+Attn backbone that attains the +8.14 pp gain is the *smallest* (0.128 M params) and runs at 2.73 ms/sample CPU inference — within the <10 ms real-time HAR budget on commodity mobile devices. The CBAM+ResNet18 control used in C10 is 89× larger but only 6.5× slower, confirming that capacity does not linearly translate to latency. The audit is reproducible on a single consumer GPU in ~5 GPU-hours; all code, per-seed accuracies, statistical pipeline, and trained model checkpoints will be released under MIT license upon acceptance.

NOVELTY AND PRIOR WORK

To our knowledge, no prior work has combined (i) paired LODO evaluation, (ii) Hedges' g-corrected effect size with CI, (iii) MDE pre-registration at fixed power, AND (iv) closed-form statistical foundations, applied to WiFi CSI cross-domain recognition. The cross-dataset replication revealing dataset-conditioned optimal representation (a four-data-point spectrum from large-effect to no-effect) is also novel. Our v2 cache reanalysis (16,447 real MMFi segments) corrects a previously-undocumented path-label artifact in the field's reference cache construction.

REPRODUCIBILITY AND TRANSPARENCY

All code (paired_runner, stat_eval, MDE calculator, t-SNE diagnostic, latency benchmark), per-seed accuracies, statistical pipeline, and trained model checkpoints will be released under an MIT license at a public repository upon acceptance. The audit is reproducible on a single consumer GPU in ~5 GPU-hours, enabling direct replication by reviewers. The pre-print of this manuscript is available at arXiv (v9, Sep 29 2026) and an anonymized version of the latest revision is in the supplementary material for double-blind review.

We note that we explicitly do NOT interpret non-significant comparisons as evidence that the underlying algorithmic idea is incorrect; we report them as "not distinguishable from noise in our paired evaluation." This framing is consistent with the IEEE TMC convention for negative results.
