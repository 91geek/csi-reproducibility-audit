ABSTRACT（论文摘要，必须 ≤ 1920 字符）
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WiFi CSI-based cross-domain gesture recognition has attracted intensive research in the past six years, yet reported improvements rarely reproduce under controlled evaluation. We present a paired benchmark covering ten recent algorithmic improvements — normalization, alignment, augmentation, reweighting, self-supervised pretraining, domain adversarial training, snapshot ensemble, and novel architectures — evaluated under an 18-fold x 5-seed cross-validated protocol on the Widar3.0 benchmark. All ten methods fail to reach statistical significance (|Cohen's d| < 0.3, p > 0.28). Variance decomposition reveals the root cause: within-seed standard deviation (4.82pp) is 4.7x the largest reported effect, guaranteeing non-reproducible "significant" results in single-seed studies. We then introduce a minimal signal-representation modification — expanding the BVP operator to retain the multi-Rx antenna dimension — that yields +8.14pp (Cohen's d = 1.71, 19/1 wins). We further test this finding on a second dataset MMFi (27 classes, 4 environments) and find it does not generalize; the dataset-optimal representation flips to multi-scale on MMFi (+1.20pp, d = 0.59, p = 0.016). t-SNE visualization reveals the geometric root cause: MMFi's four environments are indistinguishable in the BVP feature space, while Widar3.0 retains partial gestural clustering. No single BVP representation is universally optimal; the choice must be matched to the dataset's geometric structure. To address potential concerns about backbone capacity, we additionally re-implement Liu et al. 2025's CBAM+ResNet18 backbone (11.42M params, 67x our LeNetCSI_Attn) and evaluate it under our 18 paired x LODO protocol (6 fold x 3 seed). Result: Delta = -4.61pp, 95% CI [-8.72, -0.49], p = 0.028, Cohen's d = -0.517, wins 3/18 — the 67x larger backbone actively hurts, not helps. This is direct, protocol-controlled evidence that the 97.61% cross-domain number reported by Liu et al. 2025 cannot be attributed to the CBAM+ResNet18 backbone component alone under standard paired x multi-seed x LODO evaluation. The 64.6pp gap is consistent with differences in (i) data preprocessing (Liu uses raw CSI + STFT Doppler spectrum vs our BVP processing) and (ii) the customized attention modules in Liu's full system; isolating the contribution of the backbone itself is the focus of this audit. Our findings reframe cross-domain CSI research from a model-architecture problem to a joint (representation x protocol x dataset-difficulty) problem, and provide a paired evaluation protocol that other researchers can adopt to make their own reported gains auditable.
